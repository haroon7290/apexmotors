About Apex Prime Motors

A family-run dealership built on honesty and quality

Based in Slough, Berkshire, Apex Prime Motors Ltd was founded on a simple principle: sell great cars at fair prices and treat every customer the way we would want to be treated.

We hand-select every vehicle for its condition, history and value. Each car is HPI checked, mechanically inspected and professionally prepared before it reaches our forecourt. No pressure, no hidden fees - just straightforward, honest car buying.
